FAQ
Q
Is my data stored anywhere?
No — every email and password is generated and processed locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
Q
Are the results unique?
Yes, each result is freshly generated and not reused between runs.
Q
Can I use these emails anywhere?
They're formatted as standard addresses, but deliverability depends on the engine and the receiving service — verify before relying on them.
Q
Is this app free to use?
Yes, all core features are free to use.
